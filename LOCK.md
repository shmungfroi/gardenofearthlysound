What this baseline is:
- **Ground and time:** warm black ground. Time rises from a green ground line (800) to today; storeys have a per-era scale.
- **The commons:** a central green spire on an inverted mound. The mound narrows to a gold seed (the origin). Roots in each spire's colour fan from the seed to every spire.
- **Spires:** eight in jewel colours (Keyboard #3fb6a8, Chamber #4d8fe0, Concerto #9b7be8, Sacred #e0b44d, The commons #6fbf5a, Song #e46d9c, Stage #e0604d, Orchestra #ec9640). Each grows from a root and blooms at its oldest form. The wings are balanced either side of the commons.
- **Lanterns:** uppercase Outfit names on gold points. The build checks that no name touches another name or point.
- **Links:** gold hairlines with a slight whiplash bow. Dotted = influenced; red = reacted against; green = from the commons.
- **Frame:** rail (Search, Spires, Storeys, A–Z, Guide); breadcrumb Garden › Spire › Storey › Lantern; storey gutter; spire strip; minimap; ladder; card panel.
- **Search:** find, highlight, connect, then choose.
